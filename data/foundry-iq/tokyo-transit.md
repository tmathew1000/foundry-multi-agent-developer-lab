## Shinjuku connections

Shinjuku is a major rail hub with connections to central business, shopping, and
entertainment districts. Its network reduces the need to change hotels during a
short itinerary, but the station can be difficult to navigate at peak periods.

## Asakusa connections

Asakusa has metro access and convenient links toward Ueno and eastern Tokyo.
Trips to western districts such as Shinjuku generally take longer and can
require a transfer.

## Practical guidance

Allow extra time during commuter peaks. Confirm current routes, service hours,
accessibility, and disruptions with the transport operator.
