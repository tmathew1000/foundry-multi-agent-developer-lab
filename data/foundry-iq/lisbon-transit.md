## Baixa connections

Baixa provides metro, tram, bus, and rail access near many central attractions.
Steep streets and crowded historic vehicles can affect accessibility and travel
time.

## Arroios connections

Arroios has direct metro access toward the center and airport connections with
transfers. It provides a useful balance between lodging value and mobility.

## Practical guidance

Allow additional time for hills and peak-period crowding. Confirm current
routes, service hours, accessibility, and disruptions with the operator.
