## Shinjuku

Shinjuku combines extensive evening dining with direct rail connections across
central Tokyo. The indicative workshop hotel range is EUR 150 to EUR 210 per
night. It suits first-time visitors who value convenience and late dining.

The main trade-offs are crowded stations, busy streets, and possible nighttime
noise. Travelers who prioritize quiet lodging should compare properties west of
the main entertainment district.

## Asakusa

Asakusa offers a traditional atmosphere and an indicative workshop hotel range
of EUR 110 to EUR 180 per night. It provides convenient access to historic
sites, casual dining, and riverfront walks.

Evening options are quieter than Shinjuku. Cross-city trips can require more
transfers, so Asakusa favors atmosphere and value over late-night convenience.
