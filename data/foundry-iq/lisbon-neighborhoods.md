## Baixa

Baixa is central, walkable, and convenient for first-time visitors. The
indicative workshop hotel range is EUR 130 to EUR 220 per night. Restaurants,
historic squares, and transit connections are close together.

The main trade-offs are tourism pressure and higher prices near major squares.

## Arroios

Arroios provides diverse dining and metro access with an indicative workshop
hotel range of EUR 90 to EUR 160 per night. It offers stronger value than the
historic core while remaining connected to central Lisbon.

## Data boundary

Prices are workshop planning ranges. They are not live rates or availability.
