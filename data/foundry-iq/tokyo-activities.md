## Rainy-day activities

The National Museum of Emerging Science and Innovation in Odaiba is an indoor
option for technology and science interests. Allow two to three hours plus
travel time.

The Tokyo National Museum in Ueno is another indoor option focused on Japanese
art and cultural history. Allow two to four hours.

## Evening activities

Shinjuku supports food-focused evening walks and observation-deck visits.
Asakusa provides quieter evening walks near historic streets and the river.
