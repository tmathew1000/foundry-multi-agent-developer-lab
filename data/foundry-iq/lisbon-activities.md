## Rainy-day activities

The National Tile Museum provides an indoor cultural option focused on
Portuguese decorative arts. Allow approximately two hours plus travel time.

The Calouste Gulbenkian Museum provides another indoor option with art
collections and quieter surroundings.

## Evening activities

Baixa supports central dining and short walks toward Chiado. Arroios offers
neighborhood restaurants and a more residential evening atmosphere.

## Data boundary

Confirm current opening hours, ticket prices, closures, and wait times with the
venue. This source does not contain live operational information.
